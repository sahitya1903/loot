Data Deletion Policy

When you request to delete your account, the following data will be permanently deleted:

  • Your profile information (name, email, phone number).
  • Uploaded media (photos, videos, and other files).
  • Event participation records.
  • Chat messages and other interactions.

Certain anonymized usage data may be retained for analytical purposes. Additionally, backups may take up to 30 days to be fully removed.
